# 02 — Trust the safety net

**Platform:** Windows x64  
**Needs hardware:** no  
**Time:** about 2 minutes

Before you dictate into work that matters, prove that one wrong key will not erase what you said.

## Find the words you already spoke

1. Open `History`.
2. Find the sentence from the previous stage.
3. Open the entry and confirm that the transcript is there.

History is your way back to [What did I dictate last Tuesday?](../features.md#what-did-i-dictate-last-tuesday).

## Know what Escape means

The shipped choice is `General › Cancel behavior = Finish, save to history only`. If you press Escape during a take, [Escape stops the delivery, not your words](../features.md#escape-stops-the-delivery-not-your-words).

Leave `Discard recording` alone while you are learning. Before changing it, read [What a cancel can and cannot take back](../features.md#what-a-cancel-can-and-cannot-take-back).
