# Notes

## The moment

You just finished a forty-minute call, and History holds every word of it. What you need now is the half page that matters: who decided what, the numbers, and the next steps. Writing that from the transcript yourself takes longer than the call did.

## How it fits your day

Dictate or record as usual. When a take deserves more than a transcript, make a note from it right where it sits in History, in the shape your skill asks for, and find it again later on the Notes page.

## Set it up

1. Choose the provider at `Notes › Settings › Provider and model › Provider`.
2. Paste its key at `Notes › Settings › Provider and model › API key`.
3. Keep or change the model at `Notes › Settings › Provider and model › Model`.
4. Optionally import a skill with `Notes › Settings › Skills › Import file…` and pick it at `Notes › Settings › Skills › Skill`.
5. Press `History › Recordings › Make note` on a recording and read the note under it.
