# History and recovery

## The moment

You are talking and talking when something goes wrong. A stray Escape, a crash, or a long take should not erase the discussion or prompt you were building. The same fear once made every click during a recording feel risky; recovery starts by making the take retrievable even when you stop its delivery.

## How it fits your day

Treat History as part of dictation, not as an archive you visit months later. It is where you confirm an interrupted take, listen back when the transcript is unclear, and keep the entries that must outlive routine cleanup.

## Set it up

1. Keep cancellation recoverable at `General › Cancel behavior = Finish, save to history only`.
2. Keep incremental recovery enabled at `Advanced › History › Crash-Safe Recording = On`.
3. Choose the amount of unsaved history you need at `Advanced › History › History Limit`.
4. Match audio cleanup to that choice at `Advanced › History › Auto-Delete Recordings`.
5. Open the storage location from `History › Open Recordings Folder`.
6. Make a short disposable take, press Escape, and find the result on the History page before trusting the setup with a long discussion.
