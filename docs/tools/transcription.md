# Transcription

## The moment

Your brain is faster than your hands. You press a key and start speaking so the thought can move at its own speed, without becoming a typing exercise. As more sessions and windows accumulate, this remains the center of the workflow: capture the thought first, then decide how and where it should land.

## How it fits your day

Use ordinary transcription when you want the words in the field under your cursor but still want to review them before anything is sent. It is the dependable first half of every more specialized workflow in Handy Tool, from automatic submission to remote delivery and recovery.

## Set it up

1. Bind the toggle you will use for longer thoughts at `General › Transcribe Shortcut = ctrl+space`.
2. Bind the hold-to-talk option for short thoughts at `General › Push-to-Talk Shortcut = ctrl+alt+space`.
3. Leave the shipped `General › Transcription › Transcription Mode = Post-Recording` in place while you learn the flow.
4. Choose the delivery chord expected by most Windows applications at `Advanced › Transcription › Transcribe › Paste Method = Clipboard (Ctrl+V)`.
5. Put the cursor in a disposable text field, press the Transcribe shortcut, speak, and press it again. Review the result before building the rest of your workflow around it.
