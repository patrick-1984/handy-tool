# Combine recordings settings

Open `Advanced settings › Combine recordings`. The tab sets how History joins several recordings you select and copy as one text with `Combine…`. The same controls are the last step of `Setups › History`.

Catalog: [Several takes, copied as one text](../../features.md#combine-recordings).

## Combine recordings

### Between recordings

`Advanced settings › Combine recordings › Between recordings`

What goes between two recordings in the combined text: `An empty line`, `A new line` or `A space`. **Default:** `An empty line`.

### Keep each recording's prefix and suffix

`Advanced settings › Combine recordings › Keep each recording's prefix and suffix`

Adds your Transcribe prefix and suffix (`Add text before` / `Add text after` of ordinary dictation) around every recording in the combined text, as when it was pasted. Off, the recordings are joined bare. **Default:** Off.

## Around the combined text

Your own beginning and end for the combined text as a whole, e.g. "Meeting notes:".

### Add text before

`Advanced settings › Combine recordings › Around the combined text › Add text before`

Puts your text at the start of the combined text. While it is on, a `Text to insert before` field and `New line after it` (On by default) appear. **Default:** Off.

### Add text after

`Advanced settings › Combine recordings › Around the combined text › Add text after`

Puts your text at the end of the combined text. While it is on, a `Text to append after` field and `New line before it` (On by default) appear. **Default:** Off.
