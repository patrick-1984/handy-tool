# Setups

Open `Setups`.

### Basic setup

`Setups › Basic setup`

Runs the first-start setup again: the languages you speak, three speech models suggested for them (most accurate, balanced, fastest — or all of them), the basic shortcuts with their defaults, the microphone, and a Try it box that shows your first take. A model picked there downloads in the background and is selected when it lands. `Save & next` keeps a step's choice; `Skip` moves on without saving it (shortcuts and the microphone are saved as soon as they change), and `Skip setup` leaves the rest. Its Try it step shows what the take is doing, as the pill does: recording (press the shortcut again when done), transcribing with its percentage and a bar, then the words. Its last screen offers the Appearance (marked Recommended), Post-processing and Jumper setups, all optional: the defaults already work. **Default:** shown on the first start.

Catalog: [Not a black box on first launch](../../features.md#not-a-black-box-on-first-launch).

### Appearance

`Setups › Appearance`

Runs on the page, one look per step, every choice shown side by side with its own moving preview (the real pill with its sound bars, progress and glow; the live text box with words appearing): first what the pill's parts are for (the T switches the live text box, the bars are your voice, pause, and × cancels), then the theme, where the pill appears, its size, the sound bars, the progress style, the glow (not for the circling light), the colour (six ready colours, or yours from `General › App › Progress Colour`), whether the live text box is on and what it shows (it says that with the box on the take is not transcribed again at the end: ready at once, a little less accurate), its width and text size (only while it is on), and where "Too quiet" is shown. Its last step says where the rest is (General › App for the pill, General › Transcription for the live text box's lines and fade). `Save & next` saves a step's choice; `Skip` and `Close setup` keep what was set. The live text box choices are greyed out when the dictation model runs online. Also started from the top of `General › App`. **Default:** not run.

Catalog: [A guided setup for the look, with previews](../../features.md#a-guided-setup-for-the-look).

### Post-processing

`Setups › Post-processing`

Runs on the page: what the AI should do with your words (a ready prompt or your own), which AI (a provider from the registry, with its key, address and model filled in right there; `List models` asks it), the post-processing shortcut, and a Try it that sends a sample sentence through the AI with your prompt. Turns post-processing on. Also started from the top of `Advanced settings › Post-processing`. **Default:** not run.

Catalog: [A guided setup for post-processing](../../features.md#a-guided-setup-for-post-processing).

### Jumper

`Setups › Jumper`

Windows only. The Jumper lets Handy Tool paste your dictation into the right text field for you (a chat, an email, a document, a remote session), even from another window. Runs on the page: what the Jumper is for (jump back with a key, have dictation land there, or both), whether to remember the mouse position too, `Show me the place` (a 5-second countdown, then the field in focus is remembered; `Add another place` fills the next slot), the places' Set and Jump shortcuts, and a test. "Land there" sets the finish-press jump of Record/Transcribe and Transcribe & Submit to Place 1; places are kept after a restart. Also started from the top of the `Jumper` page. **Default:** not run.

Catalog: [A guided setup for the Jumper](../../features.md#a-guided-setup-for-the-jumper).
